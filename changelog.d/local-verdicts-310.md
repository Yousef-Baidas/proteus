### Changed

- Local mode counts a human verdict only from the verdict record in `proteus-lead`, written after the permission dialog or from the human's own terminal; no store comment ever counts (#310). The stand-in's `pr merge` refuses what the policy holds and requires what the host's rules would. A recorded `ACCEPT <sha12>` is the human's act for `merge: human` and for a change to a security path, which local mode holds whatever the modes say. The record stops a sandboxed agent, not an unsandboxed one: it is a working log, not proof.
